# Model credits

The glTF files in this folder are free to use under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is not required. They were converted from Kenney's FBX kits and assigned to VIRIDITE unit and building names. If a file fails to load, the game keeps the procedural placeholder.

| --- | --- | --- |

| [Kenney Castle Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit) | CC0 | Knights and king (`rifle`, `rocket`, `marksman`, `wader`, `engineer`, `vanguard`), walls, gate, towers (`yard`, `barracks`, `tower`, `wall`, `gate`, `silo`), siege engines (`artillery`, `aa`, `missile`, `factory`) |

Team colour is a small pennant added at load time. It is not part of the Kenney files. Seat colours stay cyan, orange, purple, and yellow.
